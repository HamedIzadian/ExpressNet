Common questions

How is this different from a general-purpose chatbot?

ExpressNet AI products are structured workflow systems. Every output is grounded in your source documents, backed by citations, and designed for human review — not open-ended chat.

Can I license a single product instead of the full suite?

Yes. Each product line can be licensed individually or as a bundle, with white-label and pilot options available for partners.

How is it deployed?

The suite is built on a cloud-ready architecture — FastAPI, Docker, and Azure Container Apps — with monitoring and SaaS foundations for enterprise deployment.

Is my data used to train models?

The products are designed around grounded retrieval over your own documents with governance controls. Data handling terms are agreed per engagement — contact us for specifics.
